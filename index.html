How to use

  1. Measure the time for 20 oscillations three times.
  2. Enter length and the three measured times, or import your Excel sheet.
  3. Check the T² vs l graph and best-fit line.
  4. Use the calculated gradient to determine g.
  5. Export a CSV or backup file when finished.
